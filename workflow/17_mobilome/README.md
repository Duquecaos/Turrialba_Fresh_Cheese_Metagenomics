# Mobilome analysis

This module integrates several complementary sources of evidence for
mobile genetic elements in the shotgun metagenomic dataset.

The workflow evaluates:

- geNomad plasmid candidates;
- PlasmidFinder replicon evidence;
- antimicrobial-resistance and virulence cargo;
- IntegronFinder structures;
- ISEScan insertion sequences;
- local ARG–mobile-element contexts;
- AlienHunter composition-based candidate genomic-island regions.

Predictions from individual tools are treated as evidence for mobile
genetic-element context rather than as definitive proof of mobility or
horizontal transfer.

## Upstream dependencies

This module requires outputs from:

- the six producer-level coassemblies;
- the geNomad analysis generated by the virome workflow;
- the ABRicate screening of the 18 representative MAGs;
- the community resistome analysis;
- the final representative MAG collection and its contig traceability.

## Plasmid candidates

Step 93B consolidates the plasmid predictions generated by geNomad across
the six coassemblies and removes exact sequence redundancy.

Exact recurrence across coassemblies is not interpreted as prevalence,
and no plasmid OTUs are defined.

Step 93C0 reconstructs traceability between final-MAG contigs and their
original coassembly contigs.

Step 93C1 integrates geNomad plasmid candidates with PlasmidFinder
replicons, antimicrobial-resistance loci and virulence loci.

A geNomad plasmid prediction is not interpreted as confirmation that the
sequence represents an autonomous plasmid.

Likewise, colocalization of an ARG with a plasmid candidate does not by
itself demonstrate horizontal transfer.

Step 93C2 summarizes independent mobility and cargo evidence.

## Integrons and insertion sequences

Step 93D1 applies IntegronFinder to the six coassemblies.

Step 93D2 integrates integron structures with community ARG loci and
plasmid-candidate context.

Step 93D3 applies ISEScan to the six coassemblies and retains both
complete and partial insertion-sequence predictions.

An ISEScan prediction is interpreted as sequence evidence for an
insertion-sequence element and not as demonstrated transposition.

Step 93D4 integrates insertion sequences with ARGs, integrons, plasmid
candidates and MAG traceability.

Step 93D5 reconstructs local mobile-element contexts around ARG–IS
associations and compares sequence context among coassemblies.

## Composition-based genomic-island candidates

Step 93E2A prepares eligible sequence inputs for AlienHunter.

Step 93E2B runs AlienHunter.

Step 93E2C0 audits the complete AlienHunter output set and its feature
schema.

Step 93E2C parses the outputs into the canonical genomic-island candidate
catalog.

AlienHunter detections are referred to as
`composition_based_candidate_GI_region`.

They are not interpreted as experimentally validated genomic islands or
as direct demonstrations of horizontal gene transfer.

A negative AlienHunter result is likewise not interpreted as evidence
that genomic islands are absent.

## Final mobilome integration

Step 93E3B combines candidate genomic-island intervals with:

- ARG loci;
- insertion sequences;
- integron structures;
- reconstructed ARG–IS mobile contexts;
- plasmid-candidate context.

Integration is performed using the original coassembly and contig
coordinates whenever interval-resolved evidence is available.

The final evidence classes therefore describe combinations of genomic
context and computational evidence rather than direct observations of
DNA transfer.
