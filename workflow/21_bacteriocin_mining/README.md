# Module 21 — Bacteriocin and RiPP mining

## Scope

This module contains the final computational branch used to refine,
expand, quantify, and synthesize bacteriocin/RiPP candidate evidence
in the fresh-cheese shotgun metagenomic dataset.

It integrates:

- previously established bacteriocin candidate loci;
- existing antiSMASH, BAGEL4, and CompaRiPPson evidence;
- protein-level HMM and sequence-similarity screening;
- incomplete-bin and contig-level candidate recovery;
- targeted antiSMASH and BAGEL4 follow-up;
- sequence/context deduplication;
- competitive read recruitment across the 18 metagenomic samples;
- final evidence synthesis for thesis reporting.

Computational detection or genomic-context support is not interpreted
as functional confirmation of bacteriocin production or antimicrobial
activity.

## Main upstream evidence

The branch depends on outputs generated earlier in the workflow,
including:

- the manually curated bacteriocin candidate registry in
  `38_bacteriocin_master`;
- existing antiSMASH results for the 18 representative MAGs;
- CompaRiPPson results;
- the seven established ATTRLOC loci;
- bacteriocin-locus abundance/recruitment matrices;
- the incomplete-bin protein and contig catalogs generated by the
  incomplete-bin rescue workflow.

The manually curated candidate registry is documented separately in
the bacteriocin-discovery module.

## Corrected and recovered steps

### 98B2R

98B2R corrects the interpretation of CompaRiPPson evidence.

Only evidence present in genuine CompaRiPPson result tables is counted
as CompaRiPPson candidate-level support. Candidate registries or input
sequence files are not treated as independent positive evidence.

### 98C1R

The original 98C1 search generated the HMM and BLAST raw results.

98C1R reintegrates those existing raw outputs using corrected coverage
semantics. HMM and BLAST searches were not reexecuted.

Final lineage:

    98C1 search -> 98C1R corrected integration -> 98C2

### 98C3BR2 and 98C3BR2R

98C3BR2 executed the targeted BAGEL4 analysis.

The BAGEL computation produced usable results, but the original job
subsequently encountered a shell-level validation problem.

98C3BR2R therefore performs recovery and validation of the existing
BAGEL4 outputs without rerunning BAGEL4.

The recovered BAGEL results are consumed by 98C3C and 98C_FINAL.

The recovery scripts are retained in `recovery/` to preserve the exact
provenance of the thesis analysis.

### 98D1AR supersedes 98D1A

An initial 30-context competitive reference was constructed in 98D1A.

Before read recruitment was performed, this reference was replaced by
the corrected 98D1AR locus-window design.

The final mapping reference contains:

- 7 established ATTRLOC contexts;
- 20 additional specialized candidate contexts;
- 3 exploratory candidate contexts.

Total reference set:

    7 + 20 + 3 = 30 competitive references

The seven established ATTRLOC references preserve their original local
locus contexts.

Additional candidate contexts are represented by candidate-centered
windows with flanking sequence, reducing context-length imbalance
during competitive recruitment.

98D1A is therefore not part of the final public workflow lineage.

## Competitive recruitment

Step 98D1B performs competitive Bowtie2 mapping of all 18 metagenomic
samples against the corrected 30-reference panel.

Both unfiltered primary recruitment and MAPQ >= 10 recruitment are
summarized.

The principal operational robust-recruitment rule is:

    breadth_ALL >= 0.90
    AND
    breadth_MAPQ10 >= 0.75

High total breadth accompanied by poor retention after MAPQ filtering
is treated as evidence of ambiguous or non-discriminating recruitment.

Read depth normalized per million input read pairs is a sequencing
recruitment metric and is not interpreted as direct cell abundance.

## ATTRLOC002 and ATTRLOC006

ATTRLOC002 and ATTRLOC006 are retained as separate biological locus
hypotheses even though they contain related sequence.

They are not forcibly merged.

Competitive alignment and MAPQ filtering are instead used to identify
ambiguous recruitment between these references.

This distinction is explicitly evaluated in the final 98D1C
summaries.

## Final 98E synthesis

98E integrates:

- the final 18 x 30 competitive recruitment results;
- the 98C candidate/context synthesis;
- recurrence of established candidate sequences;
- the original seven-ATTRLOC evidence framework.

The final catalog distinguishes:

1. established ATTRLOC contexts;
2. additional specialized contexts;
3. exploratory contexts;
4. recurrence or homology to existing candidate sequences.

Additional genomic contexts containing an existing or homologous
candidate sequence are not automatically interpreted as independent
novel bacteriocins.

## Interpretation guardrails

The following rules apply throughout this module:

- antiSMASH prediction is not functional confirmation;
- BAGEL4 prediction is not functional confirmation;
- HMM-profile detection is not functional confirmation;
- sequence homology is not functional confirmation;
- read recruitment is not evidence of gene expression;
- metagenomic presence is not evidence of antimicrobial activity;
- absence from an incomplete MAG or fragmented contig is not evidence
  of biological absence;
- antiSMASH and HMM evidence derived from antiSMASH profile resources
  are not treated as fully independent method families;
- additional genomic context does not by itself establish bacteriocin
  novelty;
- taxonomic attribution from incomplete or contaminated bins requires
  additional caution.

Experimental validation is required to establish bacteriocin
production and antimicrobial activity.

## Auxiliary audit scripts

The `audit/` directory contains resource, environment, and exploratory
evidence audits that were useful during development but are not direct
dependencies of the final 98E result.

These scripts are retained for provenance and methodological
transparency.

## Recovery scripts

The `recovery/` directory preserves the BAGEL4 execution/recovery
lineage required to explain how the final validated BAGEL evidence was
obtained.

These files should not be interpreted as independent biological
analyses.

## Historical variants

Known defective, superseded, backup, truncated, and pre-fix variants
are intentionally excluded from this public workflow module.

They remain part of the private analytical history but are not required
to reproduce the final thesis analysis.
