# Read-based taxonomy and targeted taxon validation

This module performs read-based taxonomic profiling of the 18
host-depleted shotgun metagenomes and targeted validation of
health-sensitive species-level classifier signals.

The workflow combines:

- Kraken2 taxonomic classification;
- Bracken read-abundance re-estimation;
- genus-level bacterial community analysis;
- corrected repeated-measures statistical inference;
- screening of food-safety-sensitive taxonomic signals;
- competitive mapping against RefSeq chromosome-focused reference panels;
- species-level evaluation using MAPQ retention, genome breadth and
  spatial distribution of mapped reads.

Species-level classifier or mapping evidence is not interpreted as proof
of viability, pathogenic phenotype or food-safety risk.

## Input data

The primary read-level inputs are the 18 host-depleted paired-end
shotgun metagenomes generated by the host-depletion workflow.

## Main workflow

### 97A - Resource audit

`97a_read_taxonomy_audit.slurm`

Audits available Kraken2/Bracken resources and the host-depleted input
dataset before taxonomic analysis.

### 97B0 - Kraken2/Bracken environment preparation

`97b0_prepare_kraken_bracken.slurm`

Prepares Kraken2, Bracken and the PlusPF reference database.

The historical analysis required an additional recovery/finalization
step, retained under `recovery/`, because the original preparation job
failed after producing the expensive Kraken database inspection output.

`recovery/97b0r_finalize_kraken_bracken.slurm` validates the already
prepared resources and creates the shared
`kraken_bracken_environment.sh` file consumed by the 97B1 worker.

For a cleaned portable implementation, the final environment-export
logic should be incorporated directly into the canonical preparation
step rather than requiring a recovery script.

### 97B1 - Read-based classification

`97b1_kraken_bracken_array.slurm`

runs:

`97b1_kraken_bracken_worker.sh`

for all 18 samples.

Kraken2 reports are generated from host-depleted paired-end reads.
Bracken estimates are calculated at genus and species ranks using the
150-bp read distribution as the closest available distribution for
151-bp reads.

Species-level Bracken assignments are treated as screening evidence,
not definitive species identification.

### 97B2A - Taxonomic matrix construction and QC

`97b2a_taxonomy_matrix_qc.slurm`

runs:

`97b2a_taxonomy_matrix_qc.py`

This step constructs genus- and species-level master tables, separates
major taxonomic categories, performs classification QC and generates
community-composition matrices.

The primary bacterial community analysis is performed at genus level.
Species-level assignments have a secondary screening role.

### 97B2B - Community description and ordination

`97b2b_bacterial_community_statistics.slurm`

runs:

`97b2b_bacterial_community_statistics.R`

This step generates the bacterial community matrices used downstream,
alpha-diversity summaries, Bray-Curtis distances, PCoA coordinates,
descriptive figures and the original inferential analyses.

The descriptive and matrix outputs remain part of the canonical
workflow.

### 97B2BR - Corrected inferential statistics

`97b2br_statistics_correction.slurm`

runs:

`97b2br_statistics_correction.R`

This step supersedes the inferential-statistics component of 97B2B.

The correction accounts explicitly for repeated biological units in the
time analysis and evaluates producer differences using the six
independent biological units rather than treating repeated observations
as independent samples.

Therefore:

- 97B2B remains the source of community matrices, ordination,
  alpha-diversity and descriptive outputs;
- 97B2BR is the canonical source of final inferential statistics.

### 97B2C - Final read-taxonomy synthesis

`97b2c_finalize_read_taxonomy.slurm`

runs:

`97b2c_finalize_read_taxonomy.py`

This step combines the taxonomic matrices, descriptive community
outputs and corrected inferential statistics into the final read-based
shotgun taxonomy results.

It also generates a food-safety-sensitive screening table.

Taxonomic labels for potentially pathogenic taxa remain screening
signals and require targeted validation before species-level claims.

## Targeted taxon validation

### 97C1 - Direct Kraken/Bracken evidence

`97c1_direct_kraken_pathogen_evidence.slurm`

runs:

`97c1_direct_kraken_pathogen_evidence.py`

This step extracts direct classifier evidence for selected
health-sensitive taxa from the existing Kraken2 reports and Bracken
outputs.

No reclassification is performed.

### 97C2A - Validation preflight

`97c2a_pathogen_validation_audit.slurm`

runs:

`97c2a_pathogen_validation_audit.sh`

This step audits computational resources, selected sample reads and the
planned competitive-mapping strategy.

### 97C2B - Competitive RefSeq panels

`97c2b_build_reference_panels.slurm`

runs:

`97c2b_build_reference_panels.sh`

with:

`97c2b_select_refseq_references.py`

Competitive chromosome-focused reference panels are constructed from
RefSeq complete genomes for target species and selected taxonomic
neighbors.

One reference genome per species was used. This does not capture all
intraspecies genomic diversity.

### 97C2C - Competitive mapping

`97c2c_competitive_mapping.slurm`

runs:

`97c2c_competitive_mapping_worker.sh`

with the helper scripts:

- `97c2c_depth_metrics.py`
- `97c2c_species_counts.py`
- `97c2c_build_task_summary.py`

Eleven selected sample-target combinations are evaluated by competitive
Bowtie2 mapping.

Evidence is summarized using:

- target-aligned reads;
- MAPQ 10 and MAPQ 20 retention;
- target share of competitive-panel mappings;
- proper-pair support;
- genome breadth;
- mean depth;
- spatial distribution of coverage;
- target rank relative to competing species.

### Retrospective 97C2C consolidation repair

The retained historical 97C2C array and worker scripts generate one
`task_summary.tsv` per task but do not contain the post-array command
that created:

`97C2C_all_task_summary.tsv`

The original historical command could not be recovered.

A deterministic reconstruction is therefore provided in:

`reproducibility_repairs/97c2c_consolidate_task_summaries.py`

The repair reads the 11 completed per-task summaries, validates task IDs
0 through 10 and reconstructs the global table in task order.

The reconstruction was validated against the historical file used by
97C2D.

Historical global table:

- rows excluding header: 11
- completed task summaries: 11
- completion markers: 11
- SHA-256:
  `41ab7e8a177403e2d2537b899d722e0082ec778c8dcfab2dccfb8862ebf02da8`

The reconstructed file produced the identical SHA-256 and an exact
byte-for-byte match with the historical table.

This demonstrates exact reproducibility of the consolidated result, but
does not imply that the reconstructed script was the original command
used during the historical analysis.

### 97C2D - Final targeted validation

`97c2d_finalize_pathogen_validation.slurm`

runs:

`97c2d_finalize_pathogen_validation.py`

The final classification integrates Kraken2/Bracken screening evidence
with competitive-mapping evidence.

The three final evidence classes are:

- `strong_distributed_species_support`
- `localized_species_level_support_insufficient`
- `no_high_MAPQ_species_specific_support`

Strong distributed support requires substantial high-MAPQ recruitment,
retention after MAPQ filtering, broad target-genome coverage and
spatially distributed signal.

These classes describe taxonomic sequence support only.

They do not demonstrate:

- viability;
- pathogenic phenotype;
- toxin production;
- infection risk;
- regulatory non-compliance;
- food-safety risk by themselves.

Failure to obtain species-specific competitive-mapping support is also
not interpreted as proof of biological absence.

## Auxiliary environment scripts

The `environment/` directory contains the R-package installation and
environment-audit scripts used to execute the statistical analyses on
the original HPC system.

These are computational-environment support scripts rather than
biological-analysis steps.

## Historical and excluded scripts

The Kraken2/Bracken L1_1 pilot (`97b1p_*`) was used for testing before
the complete 18-sample array and is not part of the canonical public
workflow.

Files with suffixes such as `.before_*`, `.corrupt_*` and
`.truncated_*` are historical or defective variants and are excluded
from the public workflow.

The original 97B2B statistical implementation remains because its
community matrices and descriptive outputs are canonical, but its
inferential conclusions are superseded by 97B2BR.
